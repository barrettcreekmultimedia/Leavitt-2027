01 / Project objectives

Turn brand visibility into sales confidence.

Show prospective customers how Leavitt understands their operation, recommends the right equipment and supports them long after the sale. Build a cohesive public-facing identity across regions without losing the local relationships that make the business relevant.

01

Establish the brand

A unified website presence showing the people, equipment and capabilities behind Leavitt Machinery.

02

Support the sales process

Shareable content that explains consultative sales, equipment selection, service and customer outcomes.

03

Build regional credibility

Real customers and branch operations from BC, Ontario and Washington, presented as one connected business.

Creative direction: customer-first, not recruitment-first. Staff stories are used to demonstrate expertise and value to customers.

02 / Creative treatment

The right equipment. The right support.

A working creative direction, not a proposed replacement for Leavitt’s existing brand language.

A / Awareness

Homepage hero

30–45 seconds of high-impact machinery, people and operations. Sound-off-first visual storytelling with a clean loop and web-optimized export. Review John’s existing anniversary interview for optional narration.

B / Consideration

Who Is Leavitt?

An interview-led company film that follows the customer journey: understand the operation, recommend a solution, deliver and support it. Combine approved archive footage with newly filmed regional material.

C / Conversion

Customer proof & sales shorts

Capture the operational challenge, Leavitt’s recommendation and the outcome. Use authentic customer testimony and verified proof points to build confidence and prompt an inquiry.

Visual and editorial approach

Industrial scale balanced with human detail: wide branch and warehouse coverage, equipment in motion, diagnostic tools, hands-on service, conversations on the floor and customer applications. Natural interview audio, purposeful pacing, restrained branded typography and consistent colour finishing. Frame key shots for both widescreen and vertical use where practical.

Core messaging reflects Leavitt’s published service offering: new and used equipment, rentals, parts, service and training. Feature the most relevant services through authentic situations rather than a checklist of every division.

Customer interview framework

What operational challenge were you facing? How did Leavitt help assess it? What equipment or support was recommended? What changed after implementation? Where possible, capture substantiated outcomes such as uptime, turnaround or productivity.

Option A prioritizes two stops in Ontario and a compact flagship/customer schedule in Auburn (one or two closely located stops). Option B supports up to three stops per remote market where routing and access allow. Both retain two travel days per regional trip.

Two-person production team in every market

Both options use a two-person crew for every shoot day, capturing interview A-roll and cinematic B-roll. Option A includes four shoot days with standard equipment; Option B includes five days with three standard and two advanced equipment days. Option A budgets two drone sessions (one standard, one advanced); Option B budgets four (two standard, two advanced). Locations and flight classifications will be confirmed during planning, subject to weather, airspace, safety and authorization.

05 / Post-production

A connected library of finished assets.

01

Editorial assembly

Ingest, backup, review and organize interview and B-roll coverage. Build the launch hero first, then the company narrative and sales-focused assets.

02

Finishing

Colour grade, sound mix, music sourcing, titles, lower thirds and approved messaging. Caption social content and deliver agreed web-ready files.

03

Review & delivery

Consolidated client feedback, revision allowance and final quality control. Both options include colour-graded B-roll selects for Leavitt’s internal marketing library, with expanded regional organization and coverage in Option B.

Hero: web-ready MP4 in agreed website specifications. Company film and customer stories: 16:9 masters with caption sidecars. Social edits: captioned 16:9 and 9:16 in Option A; 16:9, 9:16 and 4:5 in Option B. Alternate aspect ratios are reframed versions of the same creative edit, not separate concepts. Additional versions can be quoted.

Motion design

Option A includes branded animated titles, lower thirds, service callouts and end cards. Option B expands the motion treatment within the company film, customer stories and sales shorts: animated branch-location graphics, equipment and service callouts, verified customer results and branded calls to action, where relevant. No standalone animation is assumed. Final graphics will follow Leavitt’s brand guidelines and approved messaging.

Colour-graded footage library

Option A includes one consolidated 4K selects sequence (approximately 2–3 minutes) with the strongest usable footage from all three markets. Option B includes three separate 4K sequences (approximately 2–3 minutes each), one per market, organized around branch operations, equipment, field service, customer applications and aerial coverage as available. Shots are individually graded, generally 3–5 seconds long, delivered without music, titles or graphics in ProRes 422 HQ for future marketing use. These are curated selects, not raw footage or an exhaustive archive.

10 / Scope & next steps

Move from proposal to production.

Client inputs & approvals

Confirm brand assets, website playback specifications, sales priorities, branch/customer access, participant releases and permission to use existing footage. Provide consolidated feedback and verify all customer claims before release.

Scope guardrails

Includes the stated production days, editorial deliverables, revision allocations and estimated travel. Additional filming, revisions beyond the allowance, third-party voice talent, paid locations, drone coverage beyond the included allowance, raw footage, formats beyond those listed, expanded music rights and cross-border permit or specialist costs are outside scope unless approved separately. Music allowance is subject to intended media and license terms; paid media use to be confirmed.

Next steps

Review the proposal together to confirm alignment with Leavitt’s objectives, website timeline, sales priorities and regional production needs. Refine deliverables, coverage and investment wherever necessary, then confirm the final scope, estimate and production schedule before committing travel.
